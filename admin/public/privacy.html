1.4 Техникийн мэдээлэл

  • Алдаа мэдээлэх (Sentry): апп унасан үед алдааны мэдээлэл + таны дансны дугаар (UUID) л илгээгдэнэ. Имэйл, нэр, IP хаяг илгээгддэггүй.
  • Хэрэглээний тоо баримт (PostHog): ямар дэлгэц нээснийг, ямар үйлдэл хийснийг тоолно. Таны дансны дугаар, үүрэг (сурагч/багш), түвшин л дагалдана. Байршил тогтоох унтраалттай, дэлгэцийн бичлэг (session replay) хийдэггүй, бичсэн текстийг автоматаар цуглуулдаггүй.

SparkXP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05 · Operator: Aether Tech Core LLC (Mongolia)

Contact: aerielsporthub@gmail.com

SparkXP is a mobile app for learning English, built for Mongolian learners. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you delete it.

1. What we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Email address — to create your account, send a verification code, and reset your password.
  • Username and full name — to identify you in the app and on leaderboards.
  • Password — stored only as a bcrypt hash; we never keep the original.
  • Province / district (optional) — for local leaderboards. We do not use GPS or precise location.
  • English level (CEFR) — to suggest suitable lessons.
  • Profile picture (optional) — shown on your profile and leaderboards.

1.2 Information created by using the app

  • Learning progress: XP, streaks, level, trophies, completed lessons, quiz answers and scores.
  • Vocabulary: saved words and spaced-repetition review history.
  • Practice dates and durations.
  • If you join a teacher's class, that teacher can see your progress within the class.

1.3 When you use AI features

  • Text: messages you send the AI Buddy and its replies are stored so the conversation can continue and you can review it.
  • Voice: the microphone is used only during AI Buddy conversation or pronunciation practice. Audio is sent to a third-party service for transcription. Raw audio is not stored by default.
  • Camera: used only to scan a class join QR code. No photo is taken, stored, or transmitted.

1.4 Technical information

  • Crash reporting (Sentry): error details plus your account UUID only. No email, name, or IP address.
  • Product analytics (PostHog): which screens you open and which actions you take, tagged with your account UUID, role (student/teacher), and level. GeoIP lookup is disabled, there is no session recording, and typed text is not autocaptured.

2. Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We share it only with the providers needed to run the app:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI Buddy conversations, to generate replies and corrections.
  • Google (Gemini) — words and sentences you look up, for the dictionary and translations.
  • OpenAI — lesson-content generation (no personal data).
  • ElevenLabs — your recorded audio and text to be spoken, for speech-to-text and text-to-speech.
  • Cloudflare R2 · Cloudinary — image and audio storage and delivery.
  • Railway — server and database hosting.
  • Sentry · PostHog — as described in §1.4.
  • Expo (relaying to Apple/Google) — device push token, to deliver notifications.

These providers may operate servers outside Mongolia (mainly the USA and EU). By using the app you consent to that transfer. We will disclose data if legally required to do so.

3. Children and students

SparkXP is intended for students. Children under 13 must only create an account with the consent of a parent or guardian. Where a school or teacher enrols students, that organisation is responsible for obtaining consent.

If we learn that we collected data from a child under 13 without consent, we will delete the account. Parents and guardians may contact aerielsporthub@gmail.com to review or delete their child's data.

4. Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account from inside the app:

Profile → Settings → Delete account

After confirming with your password, the following is permanently erased: your account, XP, streak, level, trophies, saved words, review progress, lesson history, quiz attempts, AI Buddy conversations and memories, and class-join records. This cannot be undone.

Payment records (if you ever paid) are retained for accounting and dispute resolution, but are detached from your account and anonymised.

If you cannot access the app, email aerielsporthub@gmail.com and we will action the request within 30 days.

5. Retention

  • Data is kept while your account exists.
  • Deleting your account erases it immediately.
  • Short-lived caches (verification codes, daily usage counters) expire on their own within 24 hours.

6. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Sessions use JWT tokens held in the device's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore).
  • You can optionally lock the app with Face ID or a fingerprint.

No system on the internet is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

  • View and correct your data — Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete your account — see §4.
  • Turn off notifications, sound and haptics in Settings.
  • Questions or complaints — aerielsporthub@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date above and notify you in the app for significant changes.
